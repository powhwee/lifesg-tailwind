## Pitfalls reinforced this session

1. **Library measured size ≠ library visible size.** LifeSG's checkbox/radio is 32px box-model with ~22.4px visible SVG content inside it. Probing computed style gives the box; the user reads the visible weight. When they disagree, visible wins under the parity principle, and the measure-* gap goes into an allowlist with a clear VISUAL-PARITY reason. (Today: I went through three iterations — original 32+border-1, border-2 at 32, then size-6 — before landing on visual parity. Could have skipped to size-6 directly by reading the LifeSG SVG path data from the start.)

2. **Side-by-side visual review is the primary signal, not measure-*.** measure-* probe values can match while visual weights disagree (radio/checkbox); they can also disagree while visual reads identical (filter sidebar before vs after). Re-snap on every iteration, view, then commit.

3. **Stale allowlist reasons mask new regressions.** After a refactor, the existing allowlist's "why" may no longer apply. Today: FR's "ours stacks vertically vs LifeSG side-by-side" was true pre-row-layout, false after. Re-state with current rationale every sweep.

4. **A "smaller visual" isn't always a touch-target shrink.** Pagination's LifeSG demo measures 48 (matching our default), even though LifeSG's buttons read smaller visually — the gap is inner padding/font-size, not row height. Verified before reverting from compact to default.
